物語は、「あなた」と、あなたのお世話に奮闘するテレーゼの一日。

不慣れながらも一生懸命に洗顔やミルク、愛おしさからつい抱きしめすぎる彼女。

「あなたが幸せでいてくれることが、私にとっての幸せ」――。

彼女の腕の中で、心音や吐息を間近に感じながら、愛に包まれて眠りにつく。

これは、あなたがテレーゼの腕の中で、愛に包まれながら生きる、最も安らぎに満ちた日常の記録…

"Your happiness is my happiness..."

[STORY]

In a warm space like the deep sea, your mind awakens, guided by a gentle voice full of love.

The story covers a day with Teresse as she struggles to take care of "you." Despite being inexperienced, she does her best at washing your face, feeding you milk, and often hugging you a bit too tightly out of sheer affection.

"Your happiness is my happiness."

In her arms, you fall asleep enveloped in love, feeling her heartbeat and breath close by. This is a record of the days spent in the utmost peace and calm, where you live surrounded by love in Teresse's embrace...
