Welcome to the Monster Museum!

The exhibits here at the Monster Museum consist entirely of rare specimens imported from other worlds. From succubi and tentacles to plant-based monsters and even slimes—we have them all! At our museum, you are free to interact directly with the exhibits. Please enjoy this unique "inter-dimensional exchange" to your heart's content.

In each booth, you can view our main attraction: hand-drawn animations. We also feature "reminiscence scenes" that offer a deep dive into the monsters' daily lives and ecologies.

Please be advised that the museum assumes absolutely no responsibility for any incidents involving our guests. Nevertheless, we strive to provide a space so captivating that you will never want to leave.

In fact, you are more than welcome to stay here... forever.
