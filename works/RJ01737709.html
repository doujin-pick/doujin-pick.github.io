【Character Introductions (Kingdom of Elgion)】

■ Akari Ryugasaki (7th Squad)

Born into the prestigious Ryugasaki family, Akari possesses extraordinary natural talent.

However, her poor control over her magical power caused her to be treated as a failure by the main family, and she was eventually assigned to the notorious 7th Squad.

■ Arata (7th Squad)

The protagonist you play as.

Arata once dreamed of joining the First Army, the kingdom's most powerful military force. However, he possesses an unprecedented condition: a 0% compatibility rate between magic and machinery.

His encounter with the Relic Spirit Organir will change his fate forever...

■ Satsuki Kurogane (7th Squad)

■ Honoka Kitakaze (7th Squad)

Satsuki is an absurdly powerful fighter who seems to be apologizing for something at all times, while Honoka is an energetic twin-tailed woman with a talent for maintaining machinery.

Apparently, they're both senior members of the 7th Squad.

(Satsuki: Left / Honoka: Right)

■ Mitsuki Tokizaki (Marshal of the First Army)

Mitsuki is easygoing, absent-minded, and constantly late.

Despite her carefree personality, her unbelievably high compatibility with magical machinery and exceptional combat instincts have earned her the position of Marshal, making her one of the kingdom's most powerful military figures.

Somehow, this airhead is also the highest-ranking one of them all.

【Character Introductions (Fast Empire)】

■ Colonel Calistrong

(Fast Imperial Army)

A high-ranking Imperial officer infamous for being repulsive in just about every possible way, who proudly refers to himself as an "elite gentleman."

This disgusting old man relentlessly pursues the women of the 7th Squad, constantly plotting to capture them for himself.

■ General Roselia Alicel

(Fast Imperial Army)

A proud and exceptionally powerful general of the Fast Empire who has absolute confidence in her own combat abilities.

Rumor has it that she considers Marshal Mitsuki of the Kingdom of Elgion to be her greatest rival.
